High Flow Velocity Alert (> 30 ft/s)

Code minimum pipe size results in a velocity exceeding the recommended 30 ft/s threshold. Consider sizing up to the recommended size to prevent acoustic noise and regulator wear.

Medium / High Pressure System Notice

Operating pressure exceeds 0.5 psi. A line pressure regulator (MPR) is required upstream of appliance connections to reduce pressure to standard operating levels (≤ 14" w.g.).

PE Plastic Pipe Underground Requirement

Polyethylene (PE) piping is permitted for underground exterior gas mains only. PE pipe MUST NOT be installed inside buildings and requires an anodeless riser with tracer wire at all transitions to metal piping.
